# The Master and His Emissary — Iain McGilchrist
*Notes: 8 Aug – 26 Aug 2023*

Being too Cartesian - always 2 groups. People are different sometimes due to different brain configurations. All that is necessary is to treat all with respect - maybe use affection to connect. The metaphor of the title is from a story from Nietzsche about usurpation.

Attention is different for different people eg mountain to scientist, painter, climber (& miner) and it changes its nature - so attention comes before other functions. Types of attention: vigilance, sustained attention, alertness, focussed attention, divided attention. First 3 are the intensity axis of attention and rest is the selectivity axis. New experience is always dealt with 1st by RH and there is a release of noradrenaline.

My pain is felt in both hemispheres but others' only in right - so hurt I feel more now when someone is hurt on TV is wholly RH, & so is self awareness, empathy & identification with others. Language hides thoughts (vs telepathy) but gestures disclose them (LH for language & RH to see gestures). Music can take us to places (emotions/feelings) that language (LH) is too imprecise to get to. Nietzsche said contact with music brutalises & depersonalises language, while mundane use is uncommon. Mendelssohn suggested music is more definite (exact) than language.

RH is more for dealing with new experience and LH the normal, routine. Knowledge comes in two types: kennen is to know like we know a person (changing & difficult to describe) and wissen knows like a fact eg date of a battle, reductive as in breaking it down into parts. Music (and some other things) is known in kennen sense (German) - it does not just symbolise emotion (as words do) but carries it directly to unconscious mind.

Music & Maths are about relationships. Notes mean nothing on their own - it's the relationship between them and the spaces that creates the magic (ref *Tár*, with Cate Blanchett, which we saw the other night). Maths is the same. Everything that is now is dealt with by my model, and I choose one which seems best fit. LH likes model of machine to describe itself & its partner - machine is understood in terms of its parts. Models are comparisons, eg atom is like solar system. So he asks: can we "kennen" the hemispheres rather than "wissen" them.

Language arises from music, and music is in RH - homologates to LH language, he suggests (I think). Until recently music was a joint experience rather than solitary listening - which is why I reckon Sam Doctors' concerts are so good.

Music arose in RH as its purpose is to promote social cohesion. It is the ancestor of language, which (maybe) arose to persuade, with its models of logic. Concepts arise in creative RH thinking - which are only then transferred into language/words. Language area in LH is next to the grasping control area of the right hand - hence the relationship between language and grasping an idea: getting it, ex-PRESSing etc.

Language is I-it based rather than I-thou. It categorises and creates borders - it makes manipulation of the environment and other people (as they're not thous) possible. Hence the development of power.

Depth & primacy of implicit. Depth = context; you explore a 2D strand of "reality" in reductive LH - so first seen in context, then analysed. Feelings come before cognitive assessment; cognitive assessment can then be used to justify decisions (reasoning algorithm) - that is the primacy of affect. Affectivity is an I-thou process. Desire(?), being in the world.

The reasoning algorithm is step by step, a LH process. It is necessary for persuasion only if the other party does not have the RH overview the same as you - it must use language & be step-by-step. You can't use language to explain what is good about Biodanza, or Tai Chi, or climbing - so just say try it and see how you feel.

How does this relate to internal reasoning? Is this the process of asking the question and waiting for the answer to emerge - a RH activity - rather than making a list of pros & cons, except that, as with tossing a coin, you know which side you are "supporting."

Longing for something or someone is different from waiting - it derives from words closer to "he unite," to belonging with. For a person this is desire for affection. The skill of the artist (divine inspiration) comes from RH - it cannot be taught via a set of formal rules. Can it be learned by copying? Does the ability to sex chicken eggs come from RH? Distance. McGilchrist recommends necessary separation, and to look for depth. Myths & Metaphors are the currency of RH. LH likes the law of excluded middle - either it is or it is not.

Reason → Rationality → Reason ≡ RH→LH→RH. Boredom is lack of stimulation of LH, which itself cannot be imaginative & only has to wait for input of something new identified by RH. Propositions of the Enlightenment: all genuine questions can be answered; if a question cannot be answered it is not a question; all answers are knowable & can be discovered by means which can be learnt & taught to other people, and all answers are mutually compatible.

RH → Reason (can hold ambiguity), LH → Rationality, which underlies the 3 propositions.

RH is like a tolerant society which ends up being intolerant to the intolerant who wish to undermine it. LH seems to be the conscious mind - self conscious, its decisions seem to reflect societal norms. Unconscious is where there is desire; desire is the aim, the end, which is not a logical construct. I want to live forever (forever finishes when I die!). Being healthy feels good, but RH provides solutions to complex problems, as in I decide what I want to know & when, and leave it to unconscious (RH?). Biodanza is a striving to release the unconscious, to live a life with more feeling, more emotion.

Book is now looking at what a world without RH would be. He says it would lack wisdom - but is wisdom any more than a judgement of statistical probabilities in a very complex situation? Is this only what "AI" will do - judging likely outcomes on a very large amount of data?

In a top-down controlled society, eg Stalinism (an LH system, as is any which centralises more than necessary), quantity replaced quality, as in their rivets. But our system allows washing machines, dishwashers or lightbulbs to have built-in obsolescence. Would be nice if manufacturers had to give 10 or 20 year guarantees - would I pay for a 10 year warranty? I'd have to file the paperwork, but electronically that could easily be done. Maybe LH is short-term - and that is how we are all overshorttermised, note experiments on something today vs. tomorrow vs next year etc.

LH sees anything it can't control as being a victim! So controlling other people in COVID becomes legitimate to LH. Wisdom of "I'm not sure about this" becomes a threat. Is death a natural part of life, or a threat/lack of control - LH would see it as being a victim of...

In LH society there is obsession with certainty & security; rationality replaces reasonableness (common sense); more anger and aggression replace empathy. Victimhood is the result of not taking responsibility - taking responsibility means getting out on the pitch, not just being a spectator. Hard not to see "football hooliganism" as a striving to take responsibility for the team, rather than just watching.

As we've become a more LH society has happiness increased? No - studies show the opposite. And as Graeber said in *The Dawn of Everything*, when people have experienced both "primitive" & "modern" society and choose one, it is "primitive."

Problem with reading Heidegger - he struggles to transcend Cartesian divisions of our language. We will always struggle to say something new because language itself is a barrier, but to travel that path is the goal of human thinking.
