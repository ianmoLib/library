# Timeline

A running timeline from earliest prehistory to the present, originally started 28/7/26 and extended and amended since.

| Period | Date | Event |
|---|---|---|
| Palaeolithic | 3.3 million years ago | Earliest stone tools |
| Palaeolithic | 18,000 BC | Last Glacial Maximum — diversion of the River Severn |
| Mesolithic | 9,700 BC | Start of the Holocene, end of the Ice Age |
| Neolithic | c. 10,000–9,000 BC | Agricultural Revolution begins; Jericho founded |
| Neolithic | 6,000 BC | Pottery, irrigation, copper-working, early city-states |
| Neolithic/Bronze Age | c. 3,300 BC | Bronze Age begins |
| Bronze Age | c. 3,100–2,600 BC | Egyptian pyramids and Akkadian Empire |
| Bronze Age | 2,100 BC | Epic of Gilgamesh composed |
| Axial Age | c. 800–200 BC | Confucius, Buddha, Hebrew prophets, Zoroaster, early Greek philosophers |
| Iron Age | 1,184 BC | Traditional date, Sack of Troy |
| Iron Age | 750 BC | Iliad composed |
| Iron Age | 700 BC | Odyssey composed |
| Iron Age | c. 563 BC | Birth of the Buddha |
| Iron Age | c. 551 BC | Birth of Confucius |
| Classical | 5th century BC | Golden Age of Athens |
| — | 1st century AD | Christianity emerges |

| English royal houses | 1066 | Start of House of Normandy — Norman Conquest, William I |
