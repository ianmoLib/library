Friston — the starring role of imagination in consciousness. Imagination is how we perceive the world and predict the future, and how we conceive of Chalmers' zombies. Good feelings are about reduced uncertainty; bad ones are about increased uncertainty. To feel is more than just to respond to a stimulus.

Plants are sentient — they exhibit goal-directed behaviour, such as finding a pole to twiddle round. Descriptions of neurons tend to be just a description of all cells, of which neurons are only a special type; they communicate by electrical signals rather than chemical. For anything to be alive there must be a sense of me & not-me, with a barrier between.

We imagine what the world is like and predict what will happen given certain actions. Feelings are a response to the outside world — a certain level of surprise is good; it's all based on our model of reality. Being human, and having consciousness, is sentience with advanced features such as self-reflection, metacognition, and interiority. A world view can be reanimated with psychedelics to become more like traditional societies — our culture is unique in that, shaped by "science," sentience in nature becomes invisible.

**15/3/26 — Metonymy vs Synecdoche**

Suits = Business Execs (affecting). England = England Cricket Team (shortened description).

Do I want to lose myself — a metaphor, or dissolution of the ego — via drugs, meditation, extreme sports, sensory deprivation (float tank & darkness?), flow states, experience of awe, absorption in art? It is only through the sense of self that there is suffering.

Transcendence is to melt into pure awareness without a sense of self. We can't know what consciousness is without being able to transcend self — maybe it's a state of bliss, but it doesn't defend a self if one doesn't exist. Does a tree exist as a thing in itself in consciousness? To exist as a thing in itself it must have boundaries — but boundaries are set by consciousness as part of the process of delineating self/not-self.
