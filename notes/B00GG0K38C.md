Peter Weiss, *The Aesthetics of Resistance* Vol.1, loc 3663: "It had often happened to me that our consciousness, that every concentration on a specific field of interest ran into things that were connected, that were vital to that very point. How can you be an anarchist in a war? One isn't free unless one can leave (which military call desertion). Question for the Spanish Civil War.


Is the purpose of education for the working class to teach them passivity? No need for their ideas as the ruling class can deal with that.

So much art we have from medieval down to C18th is the glorification of leaders and Christian iconography to show us how to live in support of this. Little of the masses, the working people, whose art was expressed in their work and management of their tools. Painters such as Picasso, Géricault (*Raft of the Medusa*), Delacroix (*Liberty*), Goya, had to describe who they thought their subjects felt. The Pergamon Frieze shows how the Gods defeated the giants, and therefore what will happen to us, the masses, if we don't obey our "betters." The Christians of C4th used the Greek Muses to depict virtue and how we should live. Was it a great thing that Athelstan united England as its first King of the whole land - but if we cannot unite we end up suppressed by another warlord, as shown by the Spanish Civil War.

One can see the workers in art history but they're always somewhat hidden, though the workers at Pergamon showed something of the emotion of the Giants - I remember seeing that and wondering how it would have been if the battle had gone the other way.
