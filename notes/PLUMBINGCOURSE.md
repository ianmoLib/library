**7/5/25 — Fixing a sink**

1. Connect sink in place with bolt to the wall — the pedestal is only to hide the pipes, not for support. Drill into the wall and screw in 2 bolts, tightened in the middle with a spanner. Fixings should come with the sink.
2. Connect the taps and the plug hole — some compound to seal the plug joint is required.
3. Pipes need to go behind where the pedestal will cover them — will most likely need to be bent, or else use connectors with olives.

Bending the pipe: mark where the bend will start. Don't forget the metal butt that goes over the pipe when bending. The mark goes between the 1 & 5 of 15 on the bender, and you bend until the 30°, 45° or 90° mark touches the centre of the pipe.

4. To attach to the tap there's a special tap connector which needs to be soldered to the pipe — alternative is flexi connectors (not sure how these connect).
5. Must also include an isolator, which uses olives to connect.
6. To bend the pipe so it fits from tap to isolator: first put a 45° bend as near one end as possible — that end goes into the tap. Put a piece of pipe into the tap and measure the horizontal distance between it and the pipe with the isolator on — call it "d". Mark with pencil all the way round. Also need the vertical distance from tap to isolator, to cut the pipe so it fits.
