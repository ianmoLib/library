Reading sample of *Why War?*. My conjecture - successful war leads to more taxes to leaders, enabling them to enhance standard of living for subjects, and increases social cohesion as the promise of improved living brings people together - a sort of Ponzi scheme? The bigger the realm, the more conquest required. This was easier in the "Age of Empires" as European technology was more advanced than Africa, Asia and the Americas. I think this might explain why Hitler behaved as he did - 2 world wars were beneficial to Capitalists and the workers suffered. Certainly the best being killed is not a means of survival of the fittest. Arguments about schools from Pete Weiss also play here. Workers now need a higher skill base to be useful but no place for "failures" as from indigenous population, as they want higher wages than potential immigrants. The so-called far-right are actually of the left, as they're fighting for indigenous workers' rights against the Capitalists - so the need for Bangladeshi chefs is a middle-class need for better, cheaper food.

Ants: some ant communities enslave others, like warfare. What if we treat ant colonies as single organisms - this becomes one-on-one aggression rather like individual people defending their resources and trying to take those of others. So any collective - village, county, nation etc - could be viewed similarly, and social cohesion makes each group more homogeneous. Which groups do I belong to - certainly family and nation, especially English.
