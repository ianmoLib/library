Societies classified via Geert Hofstede by:
- greater/lesser equity of power
- greater/less autonomy
- more/less structured codes of behaviour
- more/less fluid roles for men & women
- short-term (self-gratifying) or long-term (self-denying gratification)

Countries relatively high on first score worse in mental illness & suicide (loc 3203-4 of McGilchrist).
